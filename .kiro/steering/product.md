# TamaGhost — Product

TamaGhost resurrects the beloved 90s Tamagotchi as a friendly ghost pet that
haunts your browser. The pet evolves with the owner's real focus and study
habits: care for it (feed / play / sleep) and complete focus sessions to earn
XP and evolve.

Core loop:
1. Adopt one pixel ghost and name it.
2. Keep three stats alive — Hunger, Happiness, Energy — they decay over real
   elapsed time, even while the tab is closed.
3. Run a focus timer (1 / 5 / 15 / 25 minutes). Finishing a session earns XP
   and restores the pet.
4. Evolve: Baby Boo (0 XP) → Young Phantom (60 XP) → Grown Specter (180 XP).

Tone: hauntingly charming Halloween, cute not scary. The ghost reacts with
short mood lines driven by its stats and the owner's focus habits.
UI language: English, so the submission reads directly for Devpost judges.
