How TamaGhost works

👻 The pet lifecycle

Hunger, Happiness and Energy decay in real time. Feed raises Hunger, Play raises Happiness (and costs a little Energy), and Sleep restores Energy at +16/hour while Hunger fades more slowly. Let a stat crash and your ghost will tell you about it — the speech bubble reacts to its stats and to how much you've focused today.

⏱ Focus = evolution

Focus sessions of 1 / 5 / 15 / 25 minutes earn +2 XP per completed minute. At 60 XP your Baby Boo becomes a Young Phantom; at 180 XP, a Grown Specter — new look, new lines, same needy ghost.

🛠 Built with

Kiro (spec-driven development — specs in /.kiro/specs/, steering in /.kiro/steering/, agent hooks on stat logic) · TypeScript · React · SQLite via Drizzle. Stat decay is computed from the stored last-tick time, so it stays honest across reloads and closed tabs in the full app.

🎃 Why a ghost?

The 90s Tamagotchi never really died — so for Kiroween it came back as one. TamaGhost is a Resurrection: the classic virtual pet, reimagined with a Halloween world and built for today's focus and study habits.
